About

Since 2001, APCV has brought together researchers across vision science from the Asia-Pacific region and beyond. The 19th meeting comes to Bangkok in 2027. We especially welcome students and early-career researchers.

Beyond talks and posters, APCV 2027 will feature hands-on workshops, a dedicated early-career program, and social events in the heart of Bangkok.

  • Perception
  • Attention
  • Visual cognition
  • Computational neuroscience
  • Cognitive neuroscience
  • Eye movements
  • Color
  • Clinical vision

Venue & what's next

Rattanawithayapat Building

Conference hall, 3rd floor · King Chulalongkorn Memorial Hospital, on Rama IV Road in central Bangkok, opposite Lumphini Park.

Easy access from both international airports (Suvarnabhumi and Don Mueang) and a short walk from MRT Si Lom and BTS Sala Daeng stations. Bangkok's food, temples and night markets are close by.

Getting there

Open in Google Maps · Rattanawithayapat Building, King Chulalongkorn Memorial Hospital

From the airports

  • Suvarnabhumi (BKK): Airport Rail Link into the city, then change to the MRT Blue Line at Makkasan/Phetchaburi for Si Lom, or to the BTS at Phaya Thai for Sala Daeng.
  • Don Mueang (DMK): SRT Red Line to Krung Thep Aphiwat, then MRT Blue Line to Si Lom.
  • Taxi: available at both airports. Allow extra time for Bangkok traffic, especially at rush hour.

Where to stay

Conference hotel rates will be announced with registration. In the meantime, these areas have many hotels at every budget:

  • Silom & Sala Daeng: walking distance to the venue
  • Sathorn: a short ride south
  • Siam & Pathum Wan: a few BTS stops away
  • Sukhumvit: on the BTS and MRT lines
